DATA PROTECTION

Privacy notice

This notice explains how CS-IRCFS handles information submitted through USSD, SMS, the field simulator, staff accounts, and public reporting pages. Pilot procedures remain subject to district data-protection approval.

Data we collect

Depending on the service used, information may include rainfall readings, crop and harvest reports, irrigation asset status, nutrition survey responses, phone numbers, account details, staff actions, and community feedback.

Grievances and anonymity

Community grievances are designed to be anonymous: they do not require or store a reporter account. Public pages must never publish names, telephone numbers, message text, or grievance identities.

Consent and communication

SMS advice, airtime incentives, account communication and cell-level notifications may be sent only under approved pilot settings. Participants can request an SMS opt-out or STOP-style subscription removal. Staff accounts require authorization and role approval.

Access and retention

Access is role-based and restricted to the information needed for a user's responsibilities. Personal data is retained only for documented operational and legal requirements; backups and exported reports follow the published retention policy.

Your choices

  • Text STOP to request removal from SMS communications where the service supports it.
  • Use the platform's opt-out controls when available.
  • Request correction, deletion, access, or export support through the district data administrator.
  • Use the USSD or SMS simulator instead of real reporting before provider approval.

Public information

The public landing page displays aggregate activity only: report totals, sector summaries, recent public labels, and platform status. Simulator records are excluded from field evidence.
