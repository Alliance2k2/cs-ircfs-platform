How advice is decided

Seven-day rainfall per sector, averaged across its rain gauges.

  • Irrigate moreBelow the dry-spell threshold: increase irrigation cycles.
  • Normal scheduleBetween the thresholds: keep the usual schedule.
  • Reduce irrigationAbove the wet-spell threshold: save water and pumping costs.
  • No readingsNo gauge reported this week. Monitors text IMVURA <mm> or use *801# option 3.

Thresholds are set by the district in the platform settings (DRY_SPELL_THRESHOLD_MM and WET_SPELL_THRESHOLD_MM).
