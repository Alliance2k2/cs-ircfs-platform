ABOUT CS-IRCFS

Evidence that helps farming communities act

CS-IRCFS is a district evidence platform for Bugesera. It brings together farmers, citizen science monitors, cooperatives, irrigation teams, and planners so that decisions are based on what communities are seeing in the field.

Listen locally

Reports are connected to sectors and cells, making local conditions visible.

Verify evidence

Structured records help teams compare observations, rainfall, harvest, and irrigation performance.

Act together

Planners can assign priorities, follow feedback, and coordinate practical responses.

Better decisions for every farming community

CS-IRCFS connects farmers, field monitors, irrigation teams, and district planners with trusted evidence from Bugesera.
