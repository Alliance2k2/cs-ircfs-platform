ABOUT CITIZEN SCIENCE & IRRIGATION

A shared evidence platform for Bugesera farmers and agriculture teams.

The Citizen Science and Irrigation platform brings together farmers, citizen science monitors, cooperative leaders, irrigation teams, and district planners. It records what communities observe in their fields, links every report to its sector and cell, and turns verified information into timely action. Teams can follow crop conditions, rainfall, water infrastructure, pests, and community feedback in one clear workspace, so local priorities are visible and decisions support stronger, more resilient harvests.

HOW IT WORKS

From a feature phone to a district decision.

No smartphone or internet is needed. Citizens report by USSD or SMS in Kinyarwanda; District Planners see the evidence, act, and send the answer back.

  1. 1

    Dial *801# or text 8448

    Farmers, Citizen Science Monitors and cooperative leaders report harvests, pests, rainfall, pump faults and concerns.

  2. 2

    Stored centrally

    Africa's Talking forwards every report to one database, linked to its sector, cell and irrigation scheme.

  3. 3

    Verified and prioritised

    Planners compare harvests with PADAB and APEFA Solar targets, and urgent faults become cases automatically.

  4. 4

    Answered by SMS

    Irrigation advice, local tips, airtime rewards, and closing-the-loop messages go back to the community.

WHAT WE SUPPORT

One place for the whole agriculture picture.

Citizen science & food security

Harvest forecasts, SMS pest alerts with heatmaps, and a household nutrition tracker for stunting risk.

Irrigation resilience & climate

Pump and canal fault reports by bottleneck type, a citizen rain-gauge network, and automated irrigation advice.

Community feedback loops

An anonymous grievance log, including resettlement impacts, and SMS that tells each cell what action was taken.
