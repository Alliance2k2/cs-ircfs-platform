HOW IT WORKS

Simple for farmers.
Powerful for planners.

Three steps, from a farmer's phone to a district decision and back.

  1. Step 01

    Farmers report

    Dial *801# or text 8448, in Kinyarwanda, from any phone.

  2. Step 02

    The district sees it

    Each report is placed by sector and scheme. Severe pests and broken pumps open a case at once.

  3. Step 03

    The community hears back

    Irrigation advice, airtime, and an SMS saying what was done.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious about
how it works?

Short answers about reporting and privacy.

What is CS-IRCFS?+

Citizen Science for Irrigation Resilience and Climate-smart Food Security: farmers' phone reports help Bugesera District check and improve its irrigation schemes.

Do I need a smartphone or internet?+

No. Any phone works: dial *801# or text 8448. The menus are in Kinyarwanda with numbered choices.

What do I get back?+

Irrigation advice for your sector by SMS, and 100 RWF airtime for every third rain, infrastructure or nutrition report.

Who can see my information?+

Only district staff, so they can act. Grievances are anonymous: your name and number are never kept. This page shows counts only.

What happens after I report a problem?+

Severe pests and broken pumps reach the district at once. When the problem is solved, everyone in your cell gets an SMS saying what was done.
