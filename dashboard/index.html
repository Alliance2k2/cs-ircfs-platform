How it works

From a feature phone to a district decision.

Every report is linked to its sector, cell and irrigation scheme, so the evidence lands where it is needed.

  1. Report

    Citizens dial *801# or text 8448 in Kinyarwanda.

  2. Store

    Africa's Talking forwards each report to one central database.

  3. Verify

    Harvests are compared with scheme targets. Severe pests and broken pumps become cases automatically.

  4. Reply

    Advice, tips, airtime and closing-the-loop messages go back by SMS.

What it covers

One place for the whole picture.

Food security

Harvest forecasts, SMS pest alerts with a heatmap, and household stunting risk.

Irrigation & climate

Pump and canal faults by bottleneck, a citizen rain-gauge network, and irrigation advice.

Community feedback

An anonymous grievance log, and an SMS to each cell saying what action was taken.
