How it works

From a feature phone to a district decision.

A pest report takes four key presses. Every report is linked to its sector, cell and irrigation scheme, so the evidence lands where it is needed.

  1. Report

    Citizens dial *801# or text 8448 in Kinyarwanda.

  2. Store

    Africa's Talking forwards each report to one central database.

  3. Verify

    Harvests are compared with scheme targets. Severe pests and broken pumps become cases automatically.

  4. Reply

    Advice, tips, airtime and closing-the-loop messages go back by SMS.

What it covers

One place for the whole picture.

Food security

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a smartphone or internet?

No. Any mobile phone works. Dial *801# or send an SMS to 8448.

Which language does it use?

The phone menus are in Kinyarwanda, with numbered choices and no more than three steps. The website has an English and Kinyarwanda switch.

What do I get back for reporting?

An SMS with irrigation advice for your sector after each harvest or rain report, and 100 RWF airtime for every third weather or infrastructure report.

Who can see my information?

District staff see reports so they can act on them. Grievances are anonymous: only the cell is kept, never who sent it. This public page shows counts and sectors only.

What happens after I report a problem?

Severe pests and broken pumps become cases for District Planners straight away. When a problem is solved, everyone registered in that cell gets an SMS saying what was done.

How do I become a Citizen Science Monitor?

Create an account on this site. Cooperatives can also nominate Data Champions, who report rainfall and field conditions regularly.
