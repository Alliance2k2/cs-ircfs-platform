TECHNOLOGY THAT INCLUDES EVERYONE

Simple for farmers.
Powerful for planners.

Innovation should work where it matters most—not just where internet access is strong. CS-IRCFS is designed around simple SMS and USSD reporting, with a richer dashboard for planners and agricultural teams.

01

Farmers share observations

Short, Kinyarwanda-first menus for harvests, rainfall, infrastructure and feedback.

02

Reports become useful information

Submitted observations are organized, reviewed and connected to irrigation schemes.

03

Teams act, and communities hear back

District planners identify bottlenecks and share practical follow-up information.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious about
how it works?

Here are a few helpful answers about the concept and the proposed pilot.

✳
What is CS-IRCFS?+

CS-IRCFS is a proposed Citizen Science platform for Irrigation Resilience and Food Security. It connects community reporting with data-informed evaluation of local agricultural investments.

Do farmers need smartphones or internet?+

The platform is designed for accessible USSD and SMS channels so basic feature phones can be used. The proposed short codes are examples and require provider allocation before launch.

Is the platform already live?+

These pages illustrate the proposed product experience. Live SMS routing, verified evaluation datasets and production deployment still require implementation and pilot testing.

Where will the pilot take place?+

The proposal focuses on irrigation schemes in Bugesera District, including PADAB in the Mwesa Valley and APEFA solar-powered irrigation in Ngeruka and Mareba.
